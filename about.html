Forget reading lists

Each month, members bring one to three books they've read and enjoyed – their own copies, digital files or library finds – and pitch them to the group. If one catches your eye, borrow the book, ask for the digital file or take the reference to your library. It's how we discover new authors, share our favourites and discuss and debate them.

When and where

Once a month, sometimes more, on an evening or at the weekend – the group votes on timing. We meet in a local café or bar, or in a room above the Bridgend Centre, and may take turns hosting at home as we get to know each other.

What counts

Books by women or non-binary authors with a feminist element, as subtle or as activist as you like. A suffragette's biography counts. So does a romantasy with an unconventional world and an inspiring heroine. No genre is left out.

Who we are

We're Tiffany and Xenia, two bookworms in our 30s who have lived in Bollington for a few years and wanted to meet others here who love reading as much as we do.

Come and join us

Tell us a little about yourself and we'll let you know when the next meeting is. Once you're a member, you can borrow and rate books here.

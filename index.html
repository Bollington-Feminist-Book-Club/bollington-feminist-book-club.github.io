How it works

  1. 1

    Share a book

    Add a book from your own shelf to the catalogue. It stays yours – you're lending it, not giving it away.

  2. 2

    Borrow one

    Find something you fancy and request it. We'll put you in touch with its owner to arrange the handover.

  3. 3

    Pass it on

    Read at your own pace, rate it so others know what to pick up next, then return it.

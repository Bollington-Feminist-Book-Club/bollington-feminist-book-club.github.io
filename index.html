A welcoming space to explore intersectional feminism in literature. We share books by women and non-binary authors – fiction, memoir, poetry, sci-fi – and pass them between us.

Come and join us

We meet once a month, on an evening or a weekend, to talk about what we've been reading. In between, members lend each other books from their own shelves – add one of yours and it stays yours. No genre is left out, as long as the author is a woman or non-binary and the book has a feminist thread.
